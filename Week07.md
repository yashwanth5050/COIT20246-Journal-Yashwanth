### Objective

In this activity you were asked to view some of the management settings of a wireless access point or router and the choices that might be important to a secure and reliable wireless network.

### Work Completed

The most critical parameters seen were the SSID, the security mode, the wireless key, the frequency band, the channel, the channel width, the transmit power, the DHCP setting and the guest network settings. I'd choose WPA2 or WPA3-Personal, if it's available on the client devices and WEP would be obsolete. Try to use a long wireless password and do not reuse it with other wireless systems.

Some channels may interfere with others in the 2.4 GHz band - channel should consider any nearby networks. My advice for the 5GHZ band is to whiff a channel that is not as congested and pick a channel width that fits the environment - not the one that is automatically chose to be the widest. A separate guest network may be helpful in separating visitor devices from the LAN. Also, keep the firmware for any routers up to date and keep any features that are not necessary redundant or remote disabled!

### Interpretation

Security, performance, and coverage are crucial considerations for designing a wireless device. Trying to increase the transmit power or press the channel width is no guarantee of a better reception as neighbouring networks share the same radio spectrum.
